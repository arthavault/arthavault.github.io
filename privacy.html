ARTHAVAULT Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

ARTHAVAULT is a personal finance management application designed to help users manage their money, accounts, expenses, income, budgets, and investments securely.

Your privacy is important to us. ARTHAVAULT is designed with a privacy-first approach, keeping your personal financial information on your device.

2. Information We Store

ARTHAVAULT may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Account names and balances.
  • Income and expense records.
  • Financial transactions and transfers.
  • Monthly budgets and recurring transactions.
  • Investment and share purchase/sale records.
  • Personal profile information and profile pictures.
  • Application preferences and security settings.

This information is stored locally and is not transmitted to an external server by the application.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

ARTHAVAULT does not collect or transmit your personal financial information to external servers.

  • No financial data collection.
  • No third-party advertising.
  • No analytics-based user tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal financial information.
  • No mandatory registration or online account.

4. Offline Functionality

ARTHAVAULT is designed to work offline. Its core financial management features and built-in assistant are available without an internet connection.

5. Data Security

ARTHAVAULT provides security features including:

  • Four-digit application PIN protection.
  • Fingerprint authentication, where supported.
  • Optional password-protected backup files.
  • Local data storage.

Biometric authentication is handled through the Android operating system. ARTHAVAULT does not collect or store your fingerprint data.

6. Backup and Restore

Users can create backup files and restore their information when changing devices.

Users are responsible for keeping their backup files and passwords secure.

Backup files may be saved or shared using storage locations or services selected by the user. Such external services are subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the application.

You can manage or delete supported financial records directly within the application.

Please create a backup before uninstalling the application or resetting your device if you want to preserve your information.

Previously exported backup files are not automatically deleted when information is removed from the application.

8. Third-Party Services

ARTHAVAULT does not intentionally transmit personal financial information to third-party services.

The application does not include third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

Android system services and external storage providers may operate under their own privacy policies.

If you choose to send feedback, a bug report or a suggestion, the app opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and it contains only what you write in it.

9. Children's Privacy

ARTHAVAULT does not intentionally collect personal information from children. The application is a personal financial management tool and does not require users to submit personal information to an online service.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published through the official ARTHAVAULT website.
