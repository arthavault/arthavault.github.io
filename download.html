Before you install. ARTHAVAULT is not on Google Play. It is a new app from an independent developer, so, depending on your phone, Google Play Protect may ask to scan it ("App scan recommended") or show a warning. A scan request is not a malware detection: it means Google has not seen this app before. If your phone asks, the steps are in How to install. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere; your records stay on your phone; there are no ads and no accounts. How to install

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone with the button above. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install apps.
  2. Open the downloaded file and press Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect asks to scan or shows a warning, tap Scan app first if it is offered (Google checks the file and may then say it looks safe). If a warning without a scan option appears and you trust the developer, tap Install anyway (see the screen below). The words and position can differ a little on different phones. Do not turn Play Protect off; you only need to continue with this one app.
  4. Open ARTHAVAULT and accept the Terms to start.
  5. Moving from an older version: the current version cannot be installed over 2.3.0 or any earlier version. Make a Backup in Settings first, uninstall the old app, install this version, then Restore. The backup does not contain your PIN or security questions, so set those again. Versions 2.4.0 and later install directly over each other.
