About

About ARTHAVAULT

ARTHAVAULT is a personal finance manager for people who want one clear place for their money. It helps you record every rupee, see where it goes, plan with budgets and keep track of your shares, without sending your records to a server.

It was created by an independent developer in Pokhara, Nepal, with a simple goal: make personal financial management simpler, clearer and private.

Features

Everything you need to manage your money

What ARTHAVAULT can do today.

Dashboard and total balance

See your total balance with income and expenses for today, this week or this month.

Multiple accounts

Cash, bank, wallet and investment accounts, each with its own balance.

Income and expense tracking

Record every entry with a category, a date and a note of up to 100 characters.

Money transfers

Move money between your accounts and keep both balances correct.

Budget management

Set monthly budgets by category and watch how much you have spent.

Recurring transactions

Schedule repeating payments and income, and see them on a payment calendar.

Investments and shares

Record share purchases and sales, update prices yourself and see profit or loss. The app does not fetch live prices.

Reports and analytics

Monthly, yearly and category reports with simple charts.

Backup and restore

Save a backup file, with an optional password, and restore it on a new phone.

CSV export

Export your transactions to a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet.

Nepali BS calendar and two languages

Every date uses Bikram Sambat. Use the app in English or Nepali.

PIN and fingerprint lock

Lock the app with a 4-digit PIN and, if your phone supports it, your fingerprint.

Built-in calculator and Helper

Quick sums beside the + button, and a Helper that answers in English, Roman Nepali or Nepali.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ARTHAVAULT free?

Yes. The app is free to download and has no ads.

Does it need internet?

No. ARTHAVAULT works offline and has no internet permission, so it cannot send your records anywhere.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. It is not encrypted at rest, so keep your phone locked and make backups.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. It is shared as an APK from this official website. Always download it from this website only.

Google Play Protect asks me to scan the app. Is that normal?

ARTHAVAULT is a new app from an independent developer, so your phone may ask to scan it. Tap Scan app. If a warning appears and you trust this official website, the Download page shows the steps.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings, create a backup file and, if you like, protect it with a password. To restore it on another phone, open Settings and choose restore. Keep your backup file and password safe.

What if I forget my PIN?

The app uses the security questions you set up to help you reset the PIN. Setting up the questions carefully is important.

Does it support the Nepali calendar?

Yes. Every date uses Bikram Sambat (BS 2070 to 2090). Dates after today are not accepted for transactions.

Does it show live share prices?

No. The app works offline, so you update share prices yourself and it calculates your profit or loss.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 6.0 or newer.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use the Feedback form on this page. It opens your email app with a ready message. Nothing is sent unless you press send.
