ARTHAVAULT

Manage · Grow · Prosper

ARTHAVAULT is a private personal finance app for your Android phone. Record income, expenses, transfers, accounts and investments in one secure place. Your data stays on your phone.

Download APK

Why ARTHAVAULT

Built to make personal financial management simpler. Record every rupee, see where your money goes, and keep it private.

Accounts and transactions

Cash, bank, wallet and investment accounts with income, expense and transfer records.

Investments

Track holdings, purchases and profit or loss.

PIN and fingerprint

Lock the app with a PIN and, if your phone supports it, your fingerprint. Forgot PIN uses your security questions.

English and Nepali

Switch language in Settings. A guided app tour is included.

Built-in calculator

Quick sums beside the + button, and send the result into a new entry.

Backup and restore

Save a backup file, optionally password protected, and restore it any time.

App interface preview

Screens from the app, shown with sample data.

Benefits

What you get when you use ARTHAVAULT.

  • Private: the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your records anywhere.
  • Works offline, anywhere.
  • No account or sign-up needed.
  • No ads and no third-party tracking libraries.
  • Lock with PIN or fingerprint.
  • Back up your data to a file and restore it when needed.

Your data is stored on your phone and is not encrypted at rest, so keep your phone locked and make backups.
